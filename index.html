Aloha!

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Finance at the University of Hawai‘i at Mānoa's Shidler College of Business, where I am advised by Dr. Jiakai Chen.

  • My primary research area is corporate finance, with a focus on how artificial intelligence affects financial decision making and financial intermediation.
  • A second strand focuses on real estate and climate finance, developed through collaborations with the University of Hawai‘i Economic Research Organization and the Institute for Sustainability and Resilience.

My work has been presented at leading venues, including the Federal Reserve Board, the CESifo Venice Summer Institute, the World Congress of Environmental and Resource Economists, and the International Association for Applied Econometrics. I am scheduled to present at AREUEA–ASSA 2027.
